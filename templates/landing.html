How to Use MindCraft

A personal world crafted to your study needs using a pomodoro style timer and AI detection to pinpoint when you lose focus so your MindCart doesn’t fall too far off the tracks.

  1. Click Join study world! to get started.
  2. Add your study tasks with a title, due date, subject, and estimated time.
  3. Choose your preferred Pomodoro timer split.
  4. Click Proceed to Study Session to begin.
  5. The camera will track your focus and your gestures.
  6. Open your palm to pause and close your fist to play the timer
  7. Happy studying!

🔊 TURN YOUR VOLUME UP! MindCraft will automatically match the background music and scenery to the vibe of your tasks, and send alerts when you start to snooze.
